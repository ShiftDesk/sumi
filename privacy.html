Privacy Policy

Sumi · Last updated 16 October 2026

Sumi is a personal budgeting journal made by chmays.app, an independent developer based in Denmark. Sumi was built so that your financial life stays yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Who we are 2. Information we collect 3. Information you enter in the app 4. iCloud sync 5. Device backups 6. Notifications 7. Exporting your data 8. Optional tips 9. Sharing and selling 10. Keeping and deleting your data 11. Children 12. Your rights 13. Changes to this policy 14. Contact

1. Who we are

Sumi is developed and published by chmays.app (“we”, “us”), an independent app developer based in Denmark. For any question about privacy, you can contact us at support@chmays.app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Sumi has no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. Sumi contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party tracking tools.

3. Information you enter in the app

Everything you type into Sumi, including amounts, notes, monthly plans, savings goals, savings jars, reflections and wait list items, is stored on your device using Apple’s on-device storage. This information is never sent to us or to any server we control. We cannot see, read or access it.

4. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, Sumi uses Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your data in sync across your own devices, for example your iPhone and iPad. Your data is stored in your private iCloud database, which belongs to your Apple Account. Only you can access it. We, the developer, cannot see, read or access the data in your private iCloud database. iCloud is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn off iCloud for Sumi in iOS Settings, your data stays on your device only.

5. Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, Apple may also include Sumi’s data in your device backup, just as it does for other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy, not by us.

6. Notifications

Sumi can send you reminders, such as a daily reminder to log your spending, a Sunday check-in, a new month reminder and wait list reminders. These reminders are scheduled on your device and contain no personal data from us. You choose which reminders to turn on, and you can turn them off at any time in Sumi’s Settings or in iOS Settings. Apple’s iCloud service may also send silent background signals to your device so your data stays in sync; these are not visible to you and contain no information from us.

7. Exporting your data

You can export your entries as a CSV file. An export is only created when you ask for it, and you decide where it goes, for example to Files, Mail or another app. Once you share an export, it is handled by the app or service you send it to.

8. Optional tips

Sumi is free. You can choose to leave an optional tip in Settings to support development. Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information; Apple only tells the app that a tip was completed. Tips do not unlock any features.

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone, because we never receive it. Sumi does not use your data for advertising or profiling.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device, and in your private iCloud if sync is on, for as long as you keep it there. You can delete individual entries inside the app at any time; when iCloud sync is on, deleted entries are also removed from your other devices. Deleting Sumi from a device removes its data from that device. To remove Sumi’s data from iCloud as well, go to iOS Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Sumi’s data there. Data inside existing device backups is managed by Apple or your backup provider.

11. Children

Sumi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, you have rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Because we do not collect or store any personal data, and we cannot access your private iCloud data, there is nothing held by us to access, correct or delete. You stay in full control of everything in the app. If you have questions, contact us at support@chmays.app.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Sumi handles information, for example if a future version adds new features, we will update this policy and change the “Last updated” date above. The current version is always available inside the app and on this page.
