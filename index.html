The method

Four pillars for every expense

Kakeibo was created in 1904 by Hani Motoko, one of Japan's first female journalists. Every expense belongs to one of four pillars, so you always know where your money goes.

生

Survival

Groceries, transport, utilities and everyday essentials.

楽

Optional

Eating out, shopping, hobbies and small treats.

文

Culture

Books, courses, museums, concerts and music.

外

Extra

Gifts, repairs and the things you could not plan for.

How it works

A simple monthly rhythm

Kakeibo is not about strict rules. It is a gentle habit you repeat every month.

1

Save first

Set your income, fixed costs and savings goal before you plan anything else.

2

Write it down

Log each expense yourself. That small moment of attention is what makes kakeibo work.

3

Check in weekly

A few quiet minutes on Sunday to see how your four pillars are doing.

4

Reflect and improve

Answer the four questions at month's end and carry the lesson forward.
